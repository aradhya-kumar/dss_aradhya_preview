About the society

Raw curiosity in. Real-world intelligence out.

The Data Science Society is the student-led home for AI, machine learning and data science at Bennett University. We learn by building things, entering competitions and running workshops where nobody has to pretend they already know it all.

Our mission is to foster learning, drive innovation and promote ethical data practices. We grow a community that works through real problems together and turns data into insight.
